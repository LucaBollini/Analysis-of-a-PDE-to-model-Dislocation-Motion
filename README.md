This is the GitHub page for my Fourth Year R-Project:

**Analysis of a PDE to model Dislocation Motion.**

This project introduces a graphical model for the motion of a line dislocation with periodic boundary conditions.
A non-linear parabolic PDE is derived, and we work towards analysing the existence of solutions and convergence to the heat equation.
We briefly discuss fixed point methods for proving weak solutions exist, but do not perform them in detail.
The project concludes with a short proof that solutions do converge to solutions of the heat equation under adequate assumptions.
